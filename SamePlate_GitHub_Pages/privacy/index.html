Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026
Last updated: September 30, 2026

SamePlate is a household dinner-planning and coordination application. This Privacy Policy explains what information SamePlate collects, why it is used, how it is shared and protected, how it is stored, and the choices you have regarding your information. It applies to the SamePlate mobile application, beta versions distributed through Apple TestFlight, and SamePlate's related privacy and support pages.

SamePlate is designed to collect only the information reasonably needed to operate your account and help members of a household coordinate meals. SamePlate does not currently use third-party advertising, cross-app tracking, or behavioral analytics services such as Google Analytics, and SamePlate does not sell personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Account and profile information

When you create or use a SamePlate account, SamePlate may collect or store:

  • your email address;
  • your display name or profile name;
  • account and authentication identifiers;
  • information needed to maintain your signed-in session securely; and
  • a profile avatar image if you choose to upload one.

This information is used to create and authenticate your account, connect you to your household, display your profile to authorized household members, maintain your session, and provide account-related support.

Household information

To provide shared household features, SamePlate may store:

  • the household you belong to;
  • your household-member identifier;
  • household roles or permissions, where applicable;
  • information needed to associate members with shared household data; and
  • a household avatar image if a household member chooses to upload one.

Other authorized members of the same household may see your display name, profile avatar, household role where shown, and information you choose to share through SamePlate.

Dinner, meal, ingredient, cooking, and attendance information

SamePlate may store information that household members enter or generate for meal coordination, including:

  • dinner dates, planned meals, and planned times;
  • saved meals and their ingredients;
  • cooking responsibilities and cook assignments;
  • attendance status, such as eating, eating earlier, eating later, unsure, or not eating;
  • approximate eating or arrival times, when provided;
  • optional attendance or dinner-related notes; and
  • operational meal or ingredient review information used by the app's shared meal and grocery features.

This information is used to coordinate meals and may be visible to authorized members of the same household.

Grocery-list information

SamePlate may store grocery or shopping-list items and related information entered or generated by household members so that the household can maintain and coordinate a shared grocery list.

Household updates and messages

Where SamePlate provides household updates, chat, or messaging features, SamePlate may store the text and related information that household members choose to post. This content is intended to be visible to authorized members of the same household.

Please avoid entering sensitive personal information that is not necessary for household coordination.

Information stored on your device

To improve performance and support temporary offline use, SamePlate may store limited account, session, household, dinner, attendance, meal, grocery, synchronization, and related app data locally on your device.

When you sign out, SamePlate clears account-specific local session and identity information, relevant household caches, pending synchronization operations, synchronization state, and account-specific cached data used by the signed-in account. When account deletion is successfully completed, SamePlate performs the same local account-data purge so that the deleted account cannot continue to access its former cached household state on that device.

Some installation-level preferences that are not tied to a particular account, such as whether the welcome experience has already been shown on that installation, may remain on the device after sign-out or account deletion.

Support and beta feedback

If you contact SamePlate for support or privacy questions, SamePlate may receive the information you choose to include in your message. If you submit feedback through Apple TestFlight, Apple may provide SamePlate with beta-testing information such as feedback text, screenshots you choose to submit, device or operating-system details, session information, or crash information according to Apple's TestFlight features and privacy practices.

2. Information We Do Not Currently Use for Advertising or Tracking

SamePlate does not currently:

  • display third-party advertising;
  • sell or rent personal information;
  • use personal information for cross-app or cross-site advertising;
  • use Google Analytics or similar third-party behavioral analytics in the app;
  • use advertising SDKs or data-broker integrations; or
  • request access to precise location, contacts, health data, or payment information for its current core features.

If SamePlate's data practices materially change, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate before or when the relevant change is introduced.

3. How We Use Information

SamePlate uses information described above to:

  • provide, operate, and improve the app's core household-coordination features;
  • create and authenticate accounts and maintain account sessions;
  • connect users to the correct household and enforce household access controls;
  • display and synchronize shared dinner, meal, ingredient, attendance, cooking, note, update/message, and grocery information;
  • support temporary offline use and synchronize eligible changes when connectivity returns;
  • display optional profile and household avatars;
  • protect accounts, households, and the integrity and security of the service;
  • troubleshoot problems and provide support; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations when required.

SamePlate does not use household content to build advertising profiles.

4. How Information Is Shared

Within your household

SamePlate is a shared household application. Information you add to a household, such as your display name, optional avatar, dinner plans, attendance information, cooking responsibilities, notes, saved meals, grocery items, and household updates or messages, may be visible to authorized members of that household. This sharing is a core part of the service.

Service providers

SamePlate currently relies on the following providers where necessary to operate or distribute the service:

  • Supabase provides services such as authentication, database infrastructure, backend functions, synchronization-related infrastructure, and file storage for features such as avatars. Supabase may process SamePlate data on SamePlate's behalf as necessary to provide those services. Supabase's privacy information is available at supabase.com/privacy.
  • Apple provides iOS distribution and beta testing through TestFlight. Apple may process beta-testing information such as device details, app version, sessions, crashes, and feedback under Apple's own terms and privacy practices. Apple's privacy information is available at apple.com/legal/privacy.
  • GitHub Pages may be used to host SamePlate's public privacy or support pages. SamePlate does not currently add advertising trackers, behavioral analytics, or marketing cookies to those static pages. GitHub may process technical request information according to GitHub's own privacy practices.

SamePlate does not share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

Legal and security reasons

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to valid legal process, protect legal rights, investigate fraud or security issues, or protect the safety and integrity of SamePlate and its users.

5. Data Storage and International Processing

SamePlate data may be stored and processed using Supabase infrastructure in the region selected for the SamePlate project and, where necessary to provide the service, in locations where Supabase or its subprocessors operate. These locations may be outside your province, state, or country and may have different data-protection laws.

Apple, GitHub, and other independent service providers may also process information in locations described in their own privacy policies.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

SamePlate retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account and household functionality, protect the service, respond to support or security needs, and meet legitimate legal obligations where applicable.

In general:

  • account and profile information is retained while your account remains active;
  • shared household content is retained while needed to provide the household service or until it is deleted through available app controls or an applicable deletion process; and
  • infrastructure providers may retain limited backup or recovery copies for a period of time as part of routine backup, security, or disaster-recovery processes.

Deleting your account

You can initiate permanent deletion of your SamePlate account from the account area of the app. Account deletion requires an internet connection so that SamePlate can complete and verify the server-side deletion process safely.

When account deletion is successfully completed:

  • your SamePlate authentication account and profile are deleted;
  • your household membership is removed;
  • your personal attendance records, attendance notes and times, member-specific assignments, and profile avatar are deleted;
  • household updates or messages authored by you are deleted;
  • pending account-specific local synchronization work and account-specific cached data are removed from the device as part of the local purge; and
  • your local SamePlate session is cleared.

Shared household records that belong to the household rather than solely to you, such as dinner plans, saved meals, grocery items, and certain shared operational meal information, may remain available to the other household members. Where these shared records previously contained creator, cook, or reviewer attribution tied to your membership, SamePlate removes or disassociates that identifying attribution as appropriate.

A shared household avatar may remain if the household continues to exist for other members. If you are the final active member of a household, SamePlate deletes that household and its household-scoped data, including its household avatar, as part of the account-deletion process.

You may also contact SamePlate at sameplateapp@gmail.com with a privacy or deletion question. The in-app deletion control is the normal way to permanently delete an active account.

7. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information. These may include rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to raise a concern about how it is handled.

SamePlate also provides practical controls within the app for managing information where those controls are available, such as editing household content, changing attendance information, signing out, leaving a household where supported, changing an avatar, and deleting an account.

To make a privacy request or ask a question about your information, contact sameplateapp@gmail.com. SamePlate may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

8. Security

SamePlate uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information handled through the service. These include authenticated account access, encrypted network connections, database row-level access controls designed to restrict household information to authorized members, server-side privileged operations for sensitive account actions, and safeguards intended to prevent deleted accounts from continuing to access cached or shared household data.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. SamePlate therefore cannot guarantee absolute security, but the service is designed to reduce foreseeable risks and limit access to information to what is needed to operate the service.

9. Children's Privacy

SamePlate is intended for household coordination and is not designed for children to independently create and manage accounts without appropriate parent or guardian involvement. If a household includes information about a child, a parent or guardian should decide what information is appropriate to enter and share through the household.

If you believe information about a child has been collected or used inappropriately, contact sameplateapp@gmail.com so the situation can be reviewed.
